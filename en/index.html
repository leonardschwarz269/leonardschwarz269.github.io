Hermes Agent — a personal AI assistant, 24/7

A personal AI assistant running on my own VPS, available from Telegram at any moment. The core is Hermes Agent, an open-source agentic platform, with a custom Telegram router written on top in Python: 30 modules, tests, auto-start via systemd.

The assistant handles real personal and work tasks: it runs several profiles for different roles (work, finance, studies, auto business — each with its own personality and knowledge base), understands voice messages, accepts documents and photos as context, runs autonomous scheduled tasks — from server monitoring to recurring reports — works with git, and manages its own server.

Case: access without chaos. Several people use the bot → a role system (superadmin/admin/user) with newcomer approval right from the chat.
Case: files from AI straight into your hands. The agent creates reports and documents on the server → a FILE: marker automatically delivers them to Telegram as documents.
Case: 24/7 resilience. Health monitoring, auto-restart, and a safe /restart from the chat — the assistant recovers on its own, no server access needed.

AutoRadar — an AI assistant for the auto business

Not just a scraper — a professional's personal assistant: it collects car listings from marketplaces in China, Korea, the USA, and Georgia, from Telegram channels, and from any website — prices, specs, trim levels, photos → a single unified format, make/model filters, export. And it answers questions about any car like an expert.

Case: any source — one format. Every marketplace and channel has its own data structure → normalization brings everything to a single schema. Sources plug in modularly: a new marketplace or channel can be added without reworking the system.
Case: from a listing to deal economics. The collected data feeds a cost calculation tailored to the client — including delivery, customs clearance, and reconditioning. Instead of manually pricing every option — a calculation built on ready data.
Case: photos without manual downloading. The scraper pulls car photos from listings by itself — ready-made material for listing cards, posts, and client presentations.
Case: an expert friend on call. Any question about a car — the model's weak points, what to check during an inspection, comparing options — answered by the latest and fastest LLMs right in the bot. It doesn't just collect listings, it helps make decisions.
